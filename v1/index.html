Travel isn't always about going further.

Sometimes it's the small guesthouse overlooking the harbour. The morning market you stumble into before breakfast. The road you take because it looked quieter than the rest.

The places we remember are rarely defined by landmarks alone. They're shaped by where we wake up, who points us towards dinner, and the feeling that, for a little while, we've become part of somewhere else.

Not sure where to begin?

It takes surprisingly little to feel at home somewhere. A familiar path back from dinner. The café where they remember your order. The neighbour who waves every morning. Perhaps that's a better place to begin than a list of landmarks. Begin with the things that you already know you love.
